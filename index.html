About

Takshashila Vidhyapeeth

Takshashila Vidhyapeeth is committed to promoting education, creativity and cultural values. The institution encourages students to explore knowledge while developing discipline, confidence and artistic expression.


The Department of Music focuses on developing musical knowledge, performance skills, creativity and appreciation of Indian musical traditions.
